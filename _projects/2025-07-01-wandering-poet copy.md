# Translation

| **Literal translation - "The Poets Path"**                     | **As song lyrics - "The Wandering Poet"**     |
| :----------------------------------------------- | :------------------------------------------ |
| The wandering poet has his own journey           | The wandering poet walks his own road       |
| In bustling ports                                | From busy city port                         |
| In desolate canyons                              | To desolated fjord                          |
| To corners far from the hubbub                   | To corners of the world we don’t know       |
| The wandering poet has his own song              | The wandering poet sings his own song       |
| At fiery red dawn                                | From brilliant red dawn                     |
| At gentle dusk                                   | To gentle golden dusk                       |
| When faced with night, he is never silent        | When nighttime falls his voice will rise up |
| “The great world is as vast as dreams            | “The earth, vast as in our dreams           |
| The stars descend on the face of the sea         | The stars, they fall upon the seas          |
| Sailing across the roiling, boundless grasslands | When I sail across the endless green hills  |
| Who could drop their anchor?                     | Who could anchor me?                        |
| Fingers cradled by night wind                    | Fingers twining the night breeze            |
| Spirit caressed by flowing water                 | My heart cradled by the streams             |
| On those roads                                   | If you walk those roads                     |
| Perhaps they’ll remember me.                     | Maybe they would remember me.“              |
| Liang Shan Bo                                    | LSB                                         |
